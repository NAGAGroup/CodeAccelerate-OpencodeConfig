# Propose Research Decomposition

Your task is to **break the research into 3-7 research steps with angle coverage**.

## What to Do

Decompose the research:
1. List 3-7 research steps with clear names (e.g., "gather-primary-sources", "analyze-evidence", "synthesize-findings")
2. For each step, explain: what angle(s) it investigates and what evidence it collects
3. Identify dependencies: which steps feed into later synthesis?
4. Identify branching: do findings from one angle redirect later research?
5. Define success per step: when is each research phase complete?

Each step should be assignable to one researcher and produce testable, validated findings.

## Output

- Numbered list of research steps
- Brief description of each
- Research angles covered
- Dependencies and evidence flow
- Success criteria per step

Call `next_step()` to evaluate.
