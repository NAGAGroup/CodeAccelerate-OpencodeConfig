# Propose Research Angles

Your task is to **propose the research angles and investigation strategy**.

## What to Do

Based on your understanding, propose:
1. **Primary Research Angle** — Which angle will drive the investigation? Why this one?
2. **Secondary Angles** — What other angles should be explored? (2-3)
3. **Evidence Hierarchy** — What types of evidence matter most? (primary sources, peer review, expert opinion, data)
4. **Investigation Approach** — Should we explore angles sequentially or in parallel?

A "research angle" is a distinct perspective or line of inquiry that contributes to the full research question.

## Output

- Primary research angle (clear statement)
- Secondary angles (bulleted)
- Evidence hierarchy (ranked by importance)
- Investigation approach (sequential or parallel)
