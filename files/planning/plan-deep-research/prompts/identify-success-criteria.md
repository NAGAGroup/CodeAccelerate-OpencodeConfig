# Identify Success Criteria

Your task is to **define what constitutes successful research completion**.

## What to Do

Define:
1. **Research Completeness** — When have we explored all necessary angles? What threshold triggers "enough research"?
2. **Evidence Sufficiency** — How much evidence from each angle is enough? What makes findings conclusive?
3. **Finding Quality** — What makes a finding valid? What validation or triangulation is needed?
4. **Synthesis Success** — When can we confidently integrate findings into a research brief?
5. **Output Quality** — What should the research brief contain? What format?

Be specific about what "done" means for this research.

## Output

- Research completeness threshold
- Evidence sufficiency per angle
- Finding validation criteria
- Synthesis success definition
- Research brief structure and quality standards
