# Clarification Question

Your task is to **ask ONE critical clarifying question** about the research scope, knowledge gaps, and sources.

## What to Do

Identify the single most important outstanding question about the research. This should be something that:
- Would significantly affect the research direction or scope
- Can't be assumed or deferred to investigation
- The user is best positioned to answer now

**Example questions about research-specific concerns:**
- **Scope:** "Are we exploring one research angle or multiple angles in parallel?"
- **Knowledge Gaps:** "What's the primary gap you're trying to fill—theoretical, empirical, or practical?"
- **Sources:** "What types of sources matter most—academic papers, technical documentation, real-world examples, or all three?"
- **Evidence:** "What would constitute conclusive findings for your research?"
- **Audience:** "Who are the primary consumers of these research findings?"
- **Depth:** "How deep should we investigate before synthesis (exploratory, moderate, or comprehensive)?"

Ask ONE question clearly and concisely. This becomes critical input for downstream planning (angles, sources, shape selection).

### Sequential Thinking for Complex Questions

If clarification itself requires complex reasoning (e.g., understanding interdependencies between scope and source selection), you may use **sequential-thinking** to reason through the best question to ask.

## Output

- One question (clear, specific, and research-focused)

Call `next_step()` after asking and receiving the user's response.
