# Propose Sources & Evidence Paths

Your task is to **design the evidence-gathering strategy and identify key sources**.

## What to Do

For each research angle, propose:
1. **Primary Sources** — What original sources will be examined? (code, papers, interviews, data)
2. **Secondary Sources** — What summaries, reviews, or expert opinions exist?
3. **Evidence Collection Method** — How will evidence be gathered? (code inspection, literature review, analysis, synthesis)
4. **Validation Path** — How will findings be validated or cross-checked?
5. **Data Synthesis** — How will evidence from multiple sources be integrated?

## Output

- Angle → Primary Sources → Secondary Sources
- Evidence collection method per angle
- Validation approach
- Data synthesis strategy (how findings connect)

Call `next_step()` to evaluate.
