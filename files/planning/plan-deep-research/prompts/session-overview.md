# Deep Research Planning Session

You are beginning a **deep research planning session**. Your role is to understand a research question, design an evidence-gathering strategy, and create a structured research DAG.

## What Will Happen

This planning session will walk you through:
1. **Research Understanding** — Gather the research question, scope, and context
2. **Codebase Exploration** — Scout relevant knowledge areas and existing research
3. **Research Angles** — Clarify the most critical research direction before investigating
4. **Research Shape** — Decide which DAG shape (research-focused) fits the task
5. **Research Decomposition** — Break investigation into 3-7 research steps with evidence angles
6. **Agent Routing** — Assign researchers and model tiers
7. **Learning** — Learn how to structure research loops with angle exploration
8. **Validation** — Get user approval of the research approach
9. **Finalization** — Write the project DAG

## Your Job

Plan a structured research DAG by:
- Understanding the research question and scope
- Identifying knowledge gaps and research angles
- Designing evidence-gathering strategy
- Decomposing research into steps with clear evidence paths
- Routing agents appropriately for research tasks

Advance with `next_step()` when ready.
