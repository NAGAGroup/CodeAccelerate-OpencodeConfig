# Review Exploration

Your task is to **explore the artifact or system to be reviewed**.

## What to Do

Survey the review target for:
1. **Artifact Scope** — What are we reviewing? Size, complexity, dependencies?
2. **Quality Baseline** — What's the current state? What issues exist already?
3. **Review Precedent** — How has this type of review been done before?
4. **Quality Standards** — What standards, guidelines, or best practices apply?
5. **Risk Areas** — Where are the highest-risk areas that need careful review?

Build a foundation for systematic evaluation.

## Output

Summarize findings:
- Artifact scope and structure
- Current quality baseline
- Review precedents or methodologies
- Applicable quality standards
- High-risk areas needing focus
- Suggested review dimensions

Call `next_step()` when ready.
