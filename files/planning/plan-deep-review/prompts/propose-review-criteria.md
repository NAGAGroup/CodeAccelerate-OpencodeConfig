# Propose Review Criteria

Your task is to **propose the review criteria and evaluation dimensions**.

## What to Do

Based on your understanding, propose:
1. **Review Criteria** — What dimensions will be evaluated? (e.g., correctness, performance, security, maintainability, compliance)
2. **Quality Standards** — What standards or benchmarks apply to each dimension?
3. **Evaluation Method** — How will each dimension be assessed? (code inspection, testing, metrics, expert judgment)
4. **Risk Focus** — Which criteria or areas need the most scrutiny?

## Output

- Review criteria (bulleted, clear dimensions)
- Quality standards for each (specific, measurable)
- Evaluation method per criterion
- Risk-focused areas requiring deep review
