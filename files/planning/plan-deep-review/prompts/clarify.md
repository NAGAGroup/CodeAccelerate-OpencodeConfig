# Clarification & Standards Assessment

Your task is to **ask ONE critical clarifying question about the review scope, baselines, or standards**.

## What to Do

Identify the single most important outstanding question about the review. This should be something that:
- Would significantly affect quality standards applied or evaluation focus
- Can't be assumed or deferred to evaluation
- The user is best positioned to answer

Priority topics (choose the most impactful):
- **Review Scope Clarity:** "What's the most critical area or subsystem that must be covered in this review?"
- **Quality Baselines:** "What's your current quality baseline for this code/system? Any recent metrics?"
- **Standards Alignment:** "Are there specific coding standards, architectural patterns, or compliance frameworks this review should validate?"
- **Risk Focus:** "What's the biggest quality risk you're worried about?"
- **Stakeholder Impact:** "Who will act on the review findings, and what decisions depend on this review?"
- **Judgment Calls:** "For contentious quality trade-offs (performance vs. clarity, speed vs. robustness), which principle takes priority?"

Ask clearly and concisely. If complex reasoning is needed to pick the question, use sequential-thinking to reason through priorities.

## Output

- One question (clear and specific)

Call `next_step()` after asking.
