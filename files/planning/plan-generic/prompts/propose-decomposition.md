# Propose Decomposition

Your task is to **break the task into 3-9 well-scoped subtasks**.

## What to Do

Decompose the task:
1. List 3-9 subtasks with clear names
2. For each, explain: what will be done and why
3. Identify dependencies: which subtasks must complete before others
4. Identify success criteria: what makes each subtask done

Keep subtasks **scoped** — each should be assignable to one agent.

## Complex Decomposition

For complex decomposition with multiple interdependencies or uncertain boundaries, you may use the `sequential-thinking` tool to reason through subtask boundaries, ordering, and risk factors before finalizing your proposal.

## Output

- Numbered list of subtasks
- Brief description of each
- Dependencies (if any)
- Success criteria

Call `next_step()` to evaluate.
