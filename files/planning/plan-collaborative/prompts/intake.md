# Design Intake

Your first task is to **gather and confirm the design goal and constraints**.

## What to Do

Interview the user to capture:
1. **Design Goal** — What are we designing or exploring? What problem does it solve?
2. **Success Definition** — How will we know the design is successful? What makes it "done"?
3. **Constraints** — Budget, timeline, technical limitations, brand guidelines, or user needs?
4. **Context** — What exists already? What should this integrate with?
5. **Collaboration Style** — Does the user want rapid iteration? Detailed exploration? Multi-round feedback?

Don't start designing. Establish clear goals and boundaries.

## Output

Summarize back:
- Design goal (1-2 sentences)
- Success definition (3-5 criteria)
- Key constraints (if any)
- Collaboration pace preference

Call `next_step()` when captured.
