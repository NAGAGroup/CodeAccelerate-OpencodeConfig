# Context Exploration

Your task is to **explore the design context and understand relevant patterns**.

## What to Do

Survey the codebase and design space for:
1. **Existing Patterns** — What design patterns or components already exist that this should align with?
2. **Constraints from Architecture** — How does the technical architecture shape what's possible?
3. **Related Work** — Similar designs or decisions made elsewhere in the codebase?
4. **Design Precedent** — How does the product handle similar problems?
5. **User Context** — Who will use this? What's their workflow?

Build a foundation for collaborative exploration.

## Output

Summarize findings:
- Key design patterns to follow
- Architectural constraints
- 2-3 relevant precedents from the codebase
- User context summary
- Technical considerations

Call `next_step()` when ready.
