# Identify Output Artifact

Your task is to **specify the artifact that will be produced through collaboration**.

## What to Do

Define:
1. **Artifact Type** — What is the final deliverable? (design doc, prototype, specification, code, mockup, strategy)
2. **Format** — How will it be structured? (Markdown, JSON, visual file, live demo)
3. **Scope** — What does it include? What's out of scope?
4. **Update Path** — Will it be updated based on feedback or finalized?
5. **Handoff** — Who uses this artifact next? Where does it go?

Be specific about what will exist at the end of the collaboration.

## Output

- Artifact type and format
- Scope and structure
- Update/refinement path
- End-state location and usage
