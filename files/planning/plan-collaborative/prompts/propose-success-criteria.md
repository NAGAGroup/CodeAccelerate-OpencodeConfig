# Propose Success Criteria & Dialogue Shape

Your task is to **propose explicit success criteria and the collaboration shape**.

## What to Do

Based on your understanding, propose:
1. **Success Criteria** — Concrete, measurable criteria that define when the design is successful (5-7 criteria)
2. **Collaboration Shape** — Will this be rapid iteration (many short turns) or deep exploration (fewer, longer turns)?
3. **Artifact Type** — What will be produced? (design doc, prototype, specification, visual mockup)
4. **Dialogue Structure** — Will there be decision gates between iterations, or continuous feedback loops?

## Output

- Success criteria (bulleted, concrete)
- Collaboration shape (rapid, moderate, or deep)
- Primary artifact to be produced
- Proposed DAG shape (1A-linear, 1B-with-loops, 1E-user-gates)
