# Clarification Question

Your task is to **ask ONE critical clarifying question**.

## What to Do

Identify the single most important outstanding question about the design collaboration. This should be something that:
- Would significantly affect the design direction
- Can't be assumed or deferred to design iteration
- The user should answer before planning

This might be: "Who is the primary user?" or "How soon do you need a draft?" or "What's the biggest design risk you're worried about?"

Ask it clearly and concisely.

## Output

- One question (clear and specific)

Call `next_step()` after asking.
